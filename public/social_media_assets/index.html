Our system introduces an in-chat Item Review workflow: • Direct Dispatcher Chat: Direct channel between customer and dispatch team to clarify requests in real-time. • Interactive Item Check Card: Dispatchers verify and adjust store items with customers directly inside the conversation before rider dispatch. • Transparent Customer Approval: Tap 'Proceed with Changes' or 'Decline / Adjust' to ensure total agreement on what is purchased.
