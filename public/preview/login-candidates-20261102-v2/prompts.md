# Login candidates, round 2

Built-in imagegen. Concepts for user selection before implementation.

## 1. Dispatch sheet

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one finished desktop login UI screenshot for Sugo on the Go, a real staff logistics portal in Tacurong City, Philippines. Landscape 1440x900 composition. This is a restrained, professionally typeset working web interface, not a marketing landing page or a design presentation. Render the screen edge to edge without device or browser chrome. Precise proportions: regular labels 14px, body 15px, main heading 30px, inputs 48px tall, form 350px wide. Do not enlarge the form to fill the canvas. Thoughtful whitespace and careful alignment. Flat solid color, clean antialiased text, no textures.
Content: show the brand name "Sugo on the Go" exactly once, with a tiny simple delivery scooter silhouette near it. Main form heading "Sign in", supporting sentence "Use your staff account to continue." Labels "Email or username", "Password"; placeholders "Email or username" and "Enter password"; password visibility eye icon; checkbox "Remember me"; link "Forgot password?"; primary button "Sign in" with a small right arrow. Small page footer "Tacurong City" and "© 2026 Sugo on the Go". These are the ONLY words allowed. No extra wording.
Constraints: no signup, no social login, no role picker, no fabricated data, no status badges, no slogans, no repeated logos, no massive portal title, no generic hero artwork, no dashboard teaser, no card grid, no gradients, no glow, no glass, no shadows, no grain, no 3D, no decorative blobs. A coherent typographic design with deliberately restrained branding. Use understated line icons only where functional. High contrast readable text. Scooter is a small brand mark only, not a mascot.
Art direction: Swiss editorial meets a printed dispatch document, light and precise. Entire page pale paper-white #F7F7F4 with navy-black text. A slim header with brand aligned to x96 y56 and a crisp thin dark horizontal rule below at y104. Content is a horizontal composition in the middle of page, spanning x280 to1160, y235 to660. Left column x280 width245: "Sign in" at 38px in a crisp contemporary grotesk with supporting sentence below broken in two lines; no other text or imagery. A single thin vertical ink divider at x630 separates this introduction from the form at x710 width350. Right column contains the functional fields, checkbox row, button. Inputs white with visible gray outlines and 4px corners. Button solid dark navy, 48px high, square-ish corners, white text; tiny pink-red arrow at right. Accent #F62459 limited to the tiny brand mark and arrow. Footer aligned to the two outer page margins and separated by one fine rule. No enclosing card or panels. Exact alignment and tension between the compact left heading and functional right form make this look authored, calm and grounded.
```
