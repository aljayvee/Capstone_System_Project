# Login design candidates with imagery

Built-in imagegen. Preview concepts; awaiting user selection before implementation. Photography and illustration are generated concept imagery.

## 1. Local delivery

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: desktop website login screen candidate with prominent integrated imagery, full landscape 16:10 screenshot, edge-to-edge actual interface, no browser or device frame.
Primary request: redesign Sugo on the Go staff logistics portal login. User explicitly wants a visually rich image in the design because previous typography-only options looked plain. Create polished, distinctive editorial visual design grounded in everyday local delivery in Tacurong City, Philippines. Imagery must be a central part of the composition, balanced with a practical readable login form.
Brand palette: deep navy #0B132B, warm white #FAF9F6, Sugo pink-red #F62459. Compact brand name "Sugo on the Go" once, with small delivery-scooter mark. Form heading "Sign in". Helper "Use your staff account to continue." Labeled fields "Email or username" and "Password", placeholders "Email or username" and "Enter password", functional password eye icon, checkbox "Remember me", text link "Forgot password?", primary button "Sign in". Footer "Tacurong City" and "© 2026 Sugo on the Go". Exact legible spelling; no other text.
Design discipline: one neutral contemporary sans serif, 30px heading, 14px labels, approximately 380px form width and 48px input heights at a 1440px desktop width. 6px control corners. Flat UI surfaces, no gradients in UI, no glass or glow, no floating decorative UI cards, no dashboard metrics, no extra login methods, no signup, no fake claims. Image can have natural light and shading. No giant slogans or duplicate logos, no invented landmarks, no fake live map or fake data. Full finished screenshot with realistic proportions.
Image-led editorial photographic composition. Left 58% of screen is a beautiful documentary-style photograph cropped edge to edge: a Filipino delivery rider wearing a matte pink-red jacket and a proper motorcycle helmet, next to a practical dark scooter with a delivery box, outside a modest neighborhood shop on a leafy tropical Philippine street. Rider in natural candid three-quarter view, face subtle, hands anatomically accurate, no posing at camera. Late-afternoon side lighting, natural greens, pale concrete, a little roadside warmth, ordinary believable architecture, no readable shop signage. The scooter and rider occupy lower middle, top portion quiet enough for small white Sugo brand at top-left with sufficient contrast. Rich but natural photographic detail, no synthetic glossy commercial look. Right 42% warm-white uninterrupted panel, centered compact login form with dark navy type, outlined white inputs and a dark pink-red main button. City label at bottom left of photograph and legal footer at bottom of white panel. The photographic image is the main visual attraction; form is calm, compact, well-spaced.
```
